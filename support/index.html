Common questions

I'm not getting reminders

Open iOS Settings → Notifications → Homewhistle and make sure notifications are allowed. Reminders fire before the due time by your lead time (bills: a few days ahead). Items without a date, paused, or archived don't notify.

How does iCloud sync work?

When you're signed in to iCloud, Homewhistle syncs through your own private iCloud database so your data appears on your other devices. Turn it off in Homewhistle → Settings → "Sync with private iCloud"; the change applies the next time you open the app. We never have access to your iCloud data.

How do I back up or move my data?

Settings → Protect your data → Create backup, then Share to save it to Files or send it to yourself. Turn on "Encrypt backup" to protect it with a password. To restore, choose Restore backup, preview it, and pick Merge or Replace.

What does "Mark paid" do to a bill?

It records the payment and moves the bill — and its reminder — to the next due date. Homewhistle doesn't connect to your bank; amounts and dates are the ones you enter.

How do I delete an item?

On the Home tab, touch and hold an item and choose Delete. On Today, touch and hold a reminder. A message with Undo appears right after.

Face ID lock

Turn on Settings → "Require Face ID or passcode". Homewhistle locks when you leave the app and asks again when you return. If your iPhone has no passcode set, the lock can't be enforced, so the app opens normally.

The widget is empty

Open Homewhistle once so it can share your next reminders with the widget. With app lock on, medication is left out; with private notifications on, only categories are shown.

Delete all my data

Settings → Danger zone → Delete all local data removes everything on this device, including your name and photo. To also remove synced copies, turn off iCloud sync first or delete Homewhistle's data in iOS Settings → [your name] → iCloud → Manage Storage.

Not medical, financial, or food-safety advice

Homewhistle is a reminder tool. Medication reminders aren't dosing guidance, bill reminders aren't proof of payment, and best-before reminders aren't food-safety judgments.
