Homewhistle Privacy Support

Privacy Policy

Your home stays yours.

Effective date: November 7, 2026

Homewhistle ("we", "the app") is a household productivity tool built with a local-first, privacy-first design. Your data stays on your device.

Data Collection

Homewhistle collects no personal data and sends nothing to our servers. There are no analytics SDKs, no advertising trackers, and no third-party data sharing.

Data Storage

All data you enter — reminders, bills, inventory, pets, profile information, and photos — is stored locally on your device using Apple's SwiftData framework.

When you're signed in to iCloud, Homewhistle syncs your data to your own private iCloud database through Apple's CloudKit, so it appears on your other devices. You can turn this off at any time in Homewhistle → Settings → "Sync with private iCloud"; your data then stays only on the device. Only your Apple Account can access it — it is never accessible to us. Apple encrypts it in transit and at rest; if you turn on Advanced Data Protection for iCloud, it is end-to-end encrypted.

Photos

If you choose to add a profile or pet photo, it is stored only on your device and optionally synced to your private iCloud. It is never uploaded to any external server.

Notifications

Homewhistle uses Apple's local notification system to send you reminders. Notification content is generated locally and does not leave your device. You can disable notifications at any time in iOS Settings.

Home Screen Widget

If you add the Homewhistle widget, your next few reminders are shared with it through on-device storage so it can display them. While app lock is on, medication reminders are left out of the widget; with private notifications on, the widget shows only the category, not the reminder title.

Face ID / Biometric Authentication

If you enable app locking, Homewhistle uses Apple's LocalAuthentication framework to verify your identity. Biometric data never leaves your device and is handled entirely by iOS.

Backups

Homewhistle supports encrypted local backups. Backup files are created on your device and shared only by you. We have no access to your backup files.

Children's Privacy

Homewhistle does not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

Changes to This Policy
